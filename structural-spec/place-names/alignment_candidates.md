# Alignment Candidates: Distinguishing-Tier Colors → Place Names

**Status:** Speculative. Ranked by plausibility. Nothing here is asserted as decoded.

**Core problem:** Khipu record find-spots, not origins. A warehouse khipu (Incahuasi) records goods FROM elsewhere. Colors on a warehouse khipu likely encode origin, not storage location. Colors on a non-warehouse khipu may encode local identity.

---

## Tier 1: Tightest clusters (highest alignment potential)

### NB — 4 sites, 39 khipu, 1,105 cords
**Distribution:** Incahuasi 29 khipu / 1,019 cords (92%); Armatambo Huaca San Pedro 5/38; Pachacamac 1/29; Cajamarquilla 1/8.

**Reading A (warehouse origin):** Incahuasi is a storage facility in the Cañete Valley (Huarco wamani, Chinchaysuyu). If NB encodes origin of stored goods, and 92% of NB cords are at Incahuasi, NB may be:
- Goods from the local Huarco/Cañete area (simplest: local tribute stored locally)
- A major commodity stored at Incahuasi (Urton & Chu association: NB prominent in peanut khipu 1000611 [106 cords] and 1000612 [124 cords])

**Reading B (identity):** NB appears at Armatambo (Lima area) and Pachacamac too — all Chinchaysuyu coastal. If NB is a place identity, candidates: Huarco/Cañete (wamani containing Incahuasi), or a coastal Chinchaysuyu polity.

**Confidence:** Low. The commodity reading (peanuts?) competes with the place reading. Cannot distinguish on current data.

### R — 3 sites, 13 khipu, 178 cords
**Distribution:** Pachacamac 2 khipu / 125 cords (70%); Armatambo Huaca San Pedro 1/2; Nazca 1/1.

**Candidates:** Lurin wamani (home of Pachacamac oracle, Chinchaysuyu). If R encodes the Pachacamac-area identity, this is the cleanest place-name candidate in the corpus — 70% concentration at one site.

**Confidence:** Low-medium. Small sample (13 khipu). But the Pachacamac concentration is striking.

### 0Y — 1 site, 3 khipu, 15 cords
**Distribution:** Incahuasi only. 100% site-specific.

**Candidates:** Huarco/Cañete (wamani). The only color exclusive to Incahuasi. If any color encodes the warehouse's local district, this is it.

**Confidence:** Low. Only 15 cords. Could be a rare commodity, not a place.

### GG — 33 sites, 169 khipu, 1,474 cords (common distinguishing tier)
**Distribution:** Leymebamba 14 khipu / 296 cords (20%, leads); Pachacamac 29/188; Incahuasi 12/105; widespread otherwise.

**Candidates:** Chachapoya (wamani, Chinchaysuyu). Leymebamba is in the Chachapoyas region (northern highlands). GG leads at Leymebamba but is widespread — consistent with "major commodity" OR "major highland polity."

**Urton & Chu note:** GG leads in bean khipu 1000613 (33 cords). Competing commodity reading.

**Confidence:** Low. Too widespread for clean place mapping; commodity reading viable.

## Tier 2: Moderate clusters

### FB — 4 sites, 22 khipu, 249 cords
**Distribution:** Lluta Valley 1 khipu / 80 cords (32%); Incahuasi 4/31; Pachacamac 2/11; Nazca 1/1.

**Candidates:** Arica wamani (Qullasuyu). Lluta Valley is near Arica in northern Chile. A single khipu with 80 FB cords from Lluta Valley is suggestive — but n=1 khipu is thin.

**Confidence:** Very low. Single-khipu spike.

### BS — 8 sites, 20 khipu, 337 cords
**Distribution:** Lluta Valley 1/64; Pachacamac 2/52; Nazca 1/43; widespread.

**Candidates:** Same Arica/Qullasuyu area as FB, but more diffuse. Possibly related to FB (both Lluta Valley).

**Confidence:** Very low.

### BD — 2 sites, 3 khipu, 17 cords
**Distribution:** Pachacamac 1/11; Ica 1/6.

**Candidates:** Too few cords for meaningful alignment. Noted for completeness.

**Confidence:** Negligible.

### SY — 1 site + unknown, 2 khipu, 3 cords
**Distribution:** Lluta Valley 1/1; unknown 1/2.

**Candidates:** Arica area (Qullasuyu), but 3 cords is noise-level.

**Confidence:** Negligible.

## Tier 3: Common distinguishing (likely commodities, not places)

B, YB, HB, BG — widespread (18-27 sites each), high cord counts. The color-layers analysis proposed these as "major commodities." Their broad distribution argues against place identity.

- **B:** Pachacamac/Ica/Nazca spread — south-central coast commodity?
- **YB:** Pachacamac/Incahuasi/Santa/Ica — widespread staple?
- **HB:** Pachacamac leads — central coast?
- **BG:** Diffuse, many unknowns — unclear.

**Confidence:** These are probably NOT place names. Included to mark the boundary.

---

## The Santa Valley anchor

Medrano & Urton (2018) matched 6 khipu to a 1670 census of the Santa River Valley (Recuay people, San Pedro de Corongo area). Colors correlated with individuals' first names — but these are PERSONAL names in a colonial census, not place names. The Santa khipu in OKR:
- Relevance: proves color→identity mapping is possible in principle
- Limit: personal names ≠ place names; colonial ≠ Inca period
- Place context: Santa Valley, Chinchaysuyu (Santa wamani area)
