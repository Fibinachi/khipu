# Top Candidates: Same Tag Vocabulary as 1000331

**Method:** Full cord signatures `(color1, color2, knot_type, twist)` computed for all
612 khipu (54,403 cords). Scored by Jaccard similarity + weighted overlap against
1000331's 20 signatures, plus three structural markers: AB+BG compound, WWW triplet,
BG two-type split (E/Z vs L/S).

**Corpus calibration:** AB+BG appears in 7.0% of khipu (distinctive). WWW in 47.5%
(common). BG two-type split in 0.8% — only 5 khipu (very distinctive).

---

## The 5 all-three-marker khipu

### 1000331 (reference)
- Museo Amano, Lima. No provenance. 171 cords, 20 signatures.
- 13 AB+BG section terminators, 5 WWW sub-sections, BG E/Z (first half) vs L/S (second half).
- 68% of MB cords within 4 positions of a section marker (boundary labels).
- AB//L/S = 45% of cords (dominant carrier).

### 1000396 — closest match
- **Huaura Valley, near Lima (Santa Rosalia Hacienda)**. Museum für Völkerkunde, Vienna.
- 221 cords, 52 signatures. Jaccard 0.26, weighted overlap 0.96.
- 63% of cords use 1000331's shared vocabulary.
- 6 AB+BG markers, 4 WWW, BG two-type split present.
- **But:** MB is NOT a boundary label here (only 15% within 4 of markers vs 68% in 1000331).
- Carrier is split: AB//L/S 16%, MB//L/S 14.5% (co-dominant, vs 45%/7% in 1000331).
- 4 AB+BG sections with different color profiles per section.
- Verdict: same punctuation system, different document. Shared scribal tradition, not shared content.

### 1000507
- **Pachacamac, Central Coast**. Ethnologisches Museum, Berlin.
- 132 cords, 37 signatures. Jaccard 0.27, weighted 0.92.
- 1 AB+BG, 8 WWW, BG split present. 52% shared-vocabulary cords.
- No dominant carrier; MB not boundary-associated (0% within 4).
- Verdict: same tradition markers, different internal organization.

### 1000321
- Museo Temple Radicati, Lima. No provenance.
- **1,227 cords**, 144 signatures — the largest candidate by far.
- Jaccard only 0.12 but weighted overlap 0.98 (contains nearly all common 1000331 tags).
- 1 AB+BG adjacency (verified at position 866), massive W runs (667 WWW triplets).
- 73% of cords use shared vocabulary, but vocabulary is 7x larger.
- Verdict: probably a large multi-part khipu that *contains* 1000331-like material among
  other content — a filing cabinet, not the same document type. The shared tags are a
  subset of a much bigger vocabulary.

### 1000659
- **Armatambo, Huaca San Pedro**. Museo Nacional, Pueblo Libre.
- 56 cords, 28 signatures. Jaccard 0.23 but weighted only 0.25.
- Has all three markers but shares FEWER of the common tags.
- Verdict: same punctuation, different vocabulary — like using the same formatting
  toolbar to write in a different shorthand. Strongest evidence that the markers are
  a scribal *convention* independent of tag *meanings*.

## High-Jaccard without full markers

- **1000011** (Leymebamba, funerary, 44 cords): Jaccard 0.44 (highest). Small vocabulary
  (16 sigs) overlapping on generic tags. Funerary context — likely coincidental overlap
  on common signatures, not the same system.
- **1000482** (Chuquitanta, AMNH, 30 cords): Jaccard 0.37, has AB+BG but no WWW.

## Bottom line

The 5 khipu share a **punctuation system** (AB+BG terminator + BG knot-type mode split),
not a **tag vocabulary with shared meanings**. 1000396 is the closest document-level
match (Huaura Valley, 63% shared vocabulary). But tag *roles* don't transfer: MB labels
sections only in 1000331; carrier proportions differ everywhere. Same office shorthand
for punctuation, different shorthand for content — or different offices that learned
the same formatting conventions.
