## Key observations

**1000096 (Lluta Valley)** is the only khipu using more than 2 legend compounds
(5: W+KB, W+SR, W+SY, W+0G, BS+KB). But it has 2,210 cords and 22 distinct
compounds total — at that scale, 5 overlapping pairs is expected by chance,
not evidence of shared vocabulary. The compounds are scattered (max gaps
176–375 ordinals), not in definition blocks.

**Everyone else uses exactly 1 legend compound**, overwhelmingly W+KB or DB+KB.
KB is the 4th most common second color corpus-wide (3.0% of all compound cords).
A single shared +KB pair is coincidence, not a shared lexicon.

**No khipu replicates the enumeration format.** A corpus-wide SQL scan for
blocks of 4+ consecutive cords sharing color1 with distinct color2 modifiers
found exactly one khipu: 1000368 itself. The legend-as-format is unique.
