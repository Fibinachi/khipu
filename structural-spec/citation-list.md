## Methodological decisions

- 2026-10-10 (Charles): EXCLUDE funerary quipu from structural analysis. They are
  binary/categorical records (0/1 values), a different document type from quantitative
  administrative quipu. Mixing them contaminates the administrative grammar.
  Applies to Leymebamba specimens and any other funerary-context quipu.
