## The prediction vs reality

The S-twist → south coast hypothesis predicts the five unprovenanced
tied-set khipu are south coast. This is **plausible but unconfirmed**.

Against the hypothesis:
- 1000443 and 1000446 are Pachacamac (central coast, NOT south coast)
  and Z-dominant. They are in the 9-group statistically but are
  central-coast Z-twist.
- 1000608 is Incahuasi (Cañete valley, south-central) and Z-dominant.
  If S-twist = south coast, an Incahuasi khipu should be S-twist, but it isn't.

The twist-provenance mapping is not clean. Pachacamac (central) uses Z.
Incahuasi (south-central) uses Z. The tied set (unknown) uses S.

## Possible explanations

1. **The tied set is south coast.** S-twist holds, and the unknown
   provenance is south coast. Confidence: moderate. The S-twist pattern
   from the residuals analysis (38 khipu, south coast mapping) supports this.

2. **Twist is office-specific, not regional.** The tied set's office used
   S-twist; Pachacamac used Z; Incahuasi used Z. Twist marks the office,
   not the region. The south-coast correlation is because south-coast
   offices happened to use S.

3. **Twist is document-type specific.** The tied set's document type
   (multi-commodity inventory) uses S-twist regardless of region.

## The Banco Central problem

The five tied khipu (1000265-269) are at Banco Central de la Reserva,
examined by Urton and Brezine in 2004. They were NOT in the Ascher
databooks (too late). The OKR NOTES field is the only source, and it
says nothing about provenance — only that they're tied together and
nicknamed "waterfall khipu."

Without excavation context or collector records, the provenance may
be permanently unknown. The museum (Banco Central) may have acquisition
records not in OKR.

## Confidence assessment

- S-twist predicts south coast for the tied set: **moderate** (supported
  by residuals pattern, but contradicted by Incahuasi using Z)
- The tied set is a coherent office group: **high** (shared palette,
  tied together, same museum)
- 1000344 is related to the tied set: **low** (different palette,
  different twist, different structure)
- 1000443/1000446/1000608 are the same type as the tied set: **low**
  (different palettes, Z-twist, different provenances)

## Recommendation

The south-coast prediction for the tied set is worth investigating
through Banco Central acquisition records. But the 9-group as a whole
does not have a coherent provenance story — it's a statistical cluster,
not a regional group.
