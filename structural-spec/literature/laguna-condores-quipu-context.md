# Laguna de los Cóndores Quipu: Archaeological Context

## Key question
Were quipu found INSIDE mummy bundles vs separately in the burial chamber?

## Answer (from available sources)
Quipu were found ALONGSIDE the bundles in the burial chambers, NOT inside individual bundles. Multiple sources confirm quipu were tied TO EACH OTHER as a group deposit.

## Evidence

### 1. precolombino.cl (Museo Chileno de Arte Precolombino)
- "unos 225 fardos funerarios, junto a 32 quipus" — 225 funerary bundles ALONGSIDE 32 quipu
- "junto a" = next to/alongside, not inside
- Looters sacked the chullpas for weeks before authorities intervened
- One quipu has 762+ pendant cords (matches OKR 1000020, 769 cords)

### 2. "The Twisting Paths of Recall" (Urton/Salomon tradition)
- Figure 23 caption: "At Laguna de los Cóndores, some Inka-era khipus found with enshrined mummies were tied to each other."
- Khipu were TIED TO EACH OTHER — a group deposit, not individual grave goods
- "found with enshrined mummies" — in the chamber with mummies, not inside bundles

### 3. Urton 2001 argument (via Rochaix summary)
- Tomb quipu were "tomb texts" containing "information pertaining to the history of the mummies and the social groups descended from them"
- Quipu UR6 = combined biennial calendar + census of tribute payers
- Interpretation: communal historical/demographic records, not personal property

### 4. Museo Leymebamba / mincetur.gob.pe
- 219 funerary bundles, 31 quipu recovered
- Hypothesis: mummies were "antiguos administradores de la nueva provincia inca" (administrators of the new Inca province)
- If correct, quipu-keepers buried with their archive — but as a group, not individually

### 5. Guillén 2003 (PUCP Boletín de Arqueología 7:287-303)
- "la gran cantidad de quipus" confirms elite status of the burial population
- Mausoleums had two levels; roofs/balconies used for bundles of "los nuevos administradores"
- Focus is on mummification, not quipu placement specifics

## What we still don't know
- Full text of Urton 2001 (paywalled at Cambridge) — may contain bundle-by-bundle details
- Whether ANY quipu was found inside a specific bundle (vs the chamber generally)
- The looting destroyed precise spatial associations

## Bottom line
The evidence points to quipu as CHAMBER-level deposits (tied together, stored with the mummy group), not bundle-level personal items. They were the community archive, buried with the community.
