QUIPU SOURCE-CODE TRANSLITERATION
===========================================================================

LETTER KEY (common alphabet — 99.5% of knots):
  A=S-knot/S-dir  B=S-knot/Z-dir  C=L-knot/S-dir  D=L-knot/Z-dir (D4=4 turns)
  E=E-knot/S-dir  F=E-knot/Z-dir
  B? = S-knot, direction unrecorded (not rare, just incomplete data)

RARE SYMBOLS — marked «» (the 0.5%):
  «SP» spiral  «TF» tufted  «EE» double fig-8  «LL» long-long  «BL»
  «D12» = L-knot >9 turns (breaks decimal)  «D0» = L-knot, 0 turns (placeholder)
  [E] = E-knot in S-direction (valid letter, unusual direction)
  [---] = knotless cord (gap)

CHARLES'S PATTERN — [KNOWN]«UNKNOWN»[KNOWN]:
  BRACKETED = rare symbol between two common ones
  BOUNDARY  = rare symbol at edge of a common run (transition marker)
  RARE-ONLY = whole cord is rare symbols (dedicated signal cord)
